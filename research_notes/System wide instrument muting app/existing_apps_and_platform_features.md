# Existing apps and built-in phone features that mute music / remove instruments / keep voice during playback (state as of 2026-11-06)

Scope note: this covers (1) the "HaramMute" product family, (2) similar Islamic-market and general "music remover" apps/extensions, (3) Samsung Audio Eraser, Pixel Audio Magic Eraser, Apple Audio Mix / Voice Isolation, and (4) system-wide Android audio-processing apps as an existence proof. Facts older than 2026 are marked with their date where it matters.

## Key Question 1: Does Haram Mute actually remove instruments (source separation) or just mute when music is detected? Is it system-wide?

### Takeaway
HaramMute is an AI source-separation product, not a "detect music and lower the volume" tool: it uploads a YouTube link to a cloud job, produces a "clean" speech-only audio track, and plays that track in sync over the (muted) original video. It is YouTube-only, link-based, pre-processed (not real-time), server-side, and not system-wide; the mobile apps are a player for pasted/shared YouTube links and request no capture, accessibility, VPN or MediaProjection permissions.

### Inferences
- Because the clean track is generated as a whole file from a link (with a wait, internet required, 24 h cache, 15-minute length cap on the free tier, no live streams), the architecture is: server downloads YouTube audio, runs a vocal/other stem separation on GPU (Modal), caches the result, and the client plays the stem synchronized to the muted YouTube player. This is source separation, not music-detection gating.
- The optional localhost:8765-8768 permission in the Firefox add-on suggests a local companion/relay path exists or was experimented with (similar to Music Remover's design below), but nothing on the site documents it.
- The mobile app is architecturally a special-purpose YouTube player; it cannot affect Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok or any other app's audio, and has none of the permissions (RECORD_AUDIO/MediaProjection, Accessibility, VPN, notification listener) that a system-wide approach would need.
- The "instruments not detected on a recitation video" complaint is consistent with a general-purpose music/vocal separator struggling when the "music" is an isolated instrument or a sung line without a drum/bass texture.

### Gaps
- The exact separation model (Demucs/MDX/BS-RoFormer/proprietary) is undocumented; the site and policy only name the compute provider (Modal).
- Full Play Store review text could not be retrieved (Play page truncated; AppBrain returned 403). Only the three review themes above are confirmed.
- Paid plan prices on Android/web were not published on the site; only iOS IAP prices were visible.
- No Reddit or Islamic-forum discussion threads about HaramMute surfaced in searches.

## Key Question 2: Which existing app comes closest to "system-wide, real-time, remove instruments, keep voice" on Android, and what is its technique?

### Takeaway
No shipping Android app does all four at once. The closest real-time, any-app implementation is Samsung's own One UI 8.5 Audio Eraser tile (covered in KQ3), which is OEM-privileged and tuned for noise rather than music. Among third-party apps, every instrument remover (Naqi, Murem, Halal Music Remover, HaramMute, LALAL.AI, etc.) is file- or link-based and offline/batch, while every system-wide real-time audio app (RootlessJamesDSP, Wavelet, Poweramp EQ, Viper4Android, JamesDSP root) only does EQ/DSP, not separation. The architectural building blocks exist separately: on-device Demucs/MDX inference (Naqi, Murem) and AudioPlaybackCapture-based system-wide processing (RootlessJamesDSP, PhantomAmp, Equalizer314's experimental path).

**System-wide Android audio-processing apps (existence proof of processing ALL app audio) and how each attaches**

- Background (Android framework): early Android let apps with MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS attach effects to the global output mix (session 0); Google deprecated this "less than a year after Gingerbread" citing "too many problems with supporting global effects", but it was never fully removed and some devices still honour it. The sanctioned route is the ACTION_OPEN_AUDIO_EFFECT_CONTROL_SESSION broadcast carrying a session ID, which players are not required to send; since Android 8 implicit broadcasts need a context-registered receiver in a running (foreground) service. DynamicsProcessing arrived in Android 9. Effects are listed in vendor audio_effects.xml and need a native library. — [Esper: Android equalizer apps inconsistent](https://www.esper.io/blog/android-equalizer-apps-inconsistent)
- Wavelet (no root): uses DynamicsProcessing (hence Android 9+); "enhanced session detection" reads the audio service dump (AudioPlaybackConfiguration entries) using the DUMP permission granted via `pm grant` over ADB; works with most players tried "but not YouTube, which reportedly logs its session ID only for debugging"; "Legacy mode" still attempts session 0. Troubleshooting page: Legacy mode does not work over Bluetooth unless "Disable Bluetooth A2DP hardware offload" is turned on in developer options; conflicts with Samsung Sound Assistant, LineageOS AudioFX and other EQ apps; some features unavailable under the Android 15 AIDL Audio HAL; OEM battery killers stop it. — [Esper](https://www.esper.io/blog/android-equalizer-apps-inconsistent); [Wavelet Troubleshooting](https://pittvandewitt.github.io/Wavelet/Troubleshooting/)
- RootlessJamesDSP (no root, GPL): "uses Android's internal audio capture" (i.e., the AudioPlaybackCapture path) "to gain full access to the audio stream of other apps" and applies the JamesDSP engine; the README says this method "is not 100% reliable". Verbatim limitations: "Apps blocking internal audio capture remain unprocessed (e.g., Spotify, Google Chrome)"; "Cannot coexist with (some) other audio effect apps (e.g., Wavelet and other apps that make use of the DynamicsProcessing Android API)"; "Increased audio latency". Setup needs Shizuku (Android 11+) or ADB. Android 15+: must enable "Disable screen share protection" in developer options or notifications get hidden. Spotify needs a ReVanced "remove screen capture restriction" patch; apps using the native AAudio API cannot be patched. "All the limitations mentioned above are not relevant for the magisk/root version." — [RootlessJamesDSP README](https://github.com/timschneeb/RootlessJamesDSP); [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/me.timschneeberger.rootlessjamesdsp/); [morphe-spotify-capture-patches](https://github.com/aioue/morphe-spotify-capture-patches)
- RootlessJamesDSP double-audio failure mode: on custom ROMs that prevent apps from blocking capture, "RootlessJamesDSP captures itself in an infinite loop" (endless echo); workaround is to add itself to "Excluded Apps". Separate issue: audio stops after idle/pause-play cycles until processing is restarted. — [Issue #109](https://github.com/timschneeb/RootlessJamesDSP/issues/109); [Issue #355](https://github.com/timschneeb/RootlessJamesDSP/issues/355)
- PhantomAmp (unreleased, 2021 writeup): captured device audio through the screen-recording (MediaProjection) API and applied effects; drawbacks: Android 10+ only, microphone permission required, user cannot screen-record while it is active. — [Esper](https://www.esper.io/blog/android-equalizer-apps-inconsistent)
- JamesDSP (root): registers a `jdsp` library entry pointing to libjamesdsp.so and a `jamesdsp` effect with a fixed UUID in audio_effects.conf/xml, library copied to /system/lib/soundfx; note "Effect may get unloaded by Android system if no audio stream for while"; Magisk modules automate the XML edits (docs from ~2020-2022, Android 5-10 era). — [JamesDSPManager (mirror)](https://github.com/anonymix007/JamesDSPManager); [JohnFawkes JamesDSPManager module](https://www.github.com/JohnFawkes/JamesDSPManager)
- ViPER4Android FX (root): pushes libv4a_fx.so to /vendor/lib/soundfx and patches /vendor/etc/audio_effects.xml to define the v4a_fx effect; the Magisk module overlays patched audio_effects.xml and audio_policy.conf at boot, "commanding AudioFlinger to route all media audio through libv4a_fx.so". A rootless fork (RootlessViPER4Android) exists for non-rooted phones. — [Esper](https://www.esper.io/blog/android-equalizer-apps-inconsistent); [ViPER4Android FX Magisk module page](https://magisk-web.pages.dev/modules/viper4android-fx/); [RootlessViPER4Android](https://github.com/alienware377/RootlessViPER4Android)
- Poweramp Equalizer (no root): attaches per audio session; needs the DUMP permission (`adb shell pm grant com.maxmpz.equalizer android.permission.DUMP`) for "Advanced Player Tracking" of apps that "don't specially intend for their audio output to be modified by external processes", and a notification listener or Android may unload it; Android 9+ (10+ recommended); forum reports of breakage on HyperOS and conflicts with stock-ROM audio services. — [Poweramp forum: ADB permissions thread](https://forum.powerampapp.com/topic/20638-using-adb-to-allow-equaliser-permissions-for-more-audio-applications/); [Poweramp forum: HyperOS thread](https://forum.powerampapp.com/topic/27558-eq-not-working-since-my-device-updated-to-hyperos/)
- Equalizer314 by bearinmind (open source, Android 9+, on Play/F-Droid; the "Flat Equalizer" requested may be a different app, see Gaps): "System-wide EQ with multiband compression, limiter" built on DynamicsProcessing + Visualizer; uses session 0 for all apps ("only one app can control session 0 at a time", with an auto-reclaim that "sometimes fails and causes brief dropouts"); the author states the AudioPlaybackCapture route "needs ADB permissions granted through something like Shizuku", requires RECORD_AUDIO and "adds latency", while "AudioFlinger-based methods avoid latency issues but require root"; the author lists Wavelet, RootlessJamesDSP and Poweramp EQ as also contending for session 0. — [Equalizer314 on GitHub](https://github.com/bearinmindcat/Equalizer314); [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.bearinmind.equalizer314/)
- Samsung Sound Assistant: Samsung's Galaxy Store / Good Lock Labs module (com.samsung.android.soundassistant) offering per-app volume ("Individual app volumes"), 150 volume steps, floating EQ and scheduled "Scenarios"; release notes warn "due to changes in system sound processing methods, stable functionality cannot be guaranteed" and some devices are unsupported; it is OEM-privileged and its framework-level mechanism is undocumented. — [APKMirror Sound Assistant releases](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/samsung-electronics-co-ltd/soundassistant/); [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/how-to-find-and-use-samsung-sound-assistant-on-galaxy-phones/)

### Inferences
- Three attachment strategies are proven in the field: (a) session-ID effects (Wavelet, Poweramp EQ, Equalizer314): zero extra latency and no mic permission, but can only attach to sessions you can discover (needs DUMP via ADB/Shizuku to be reliable; YouTube is a known blind spot) and cannot see or rewrite raw PCM beyond what the effect API exposes, so it cannot host a neural separator; (b) AudioPlaybackCapture/MediaProjection (RootlessJamesDSP, PhantomAmp): gives raw PCM for any app that does not opt out, which is what a separation model needs, at the cost of added latency, a screen-cast permission prompt, apps that set ALLOW_CAPTURE_BY_NONE (Spotify, Chrome, SoundCloud, DRM video) being unprocessable, and an unsolved question of muting the original stream without feedback loops; (c) root/audio_effects.xml (Viper4Android, JamesDSP): the only way to intercept every stream including DRM/offloaded paths, with no latency penalty, but requires Magisk/root.
- The one documented design pairing a cheap classifier gate with a heavier model (NoNoiseYT's spectrum music detector + local speech-enhancement model; Naqi's YAMNet gate + Demucs) is the pattern a system-wide Android implementation would most plausibly copy: run detection continuously and only pay for separation while music is present.
- On-device Demucs htdemucs (84 MB fp16) is demonstrated to run on arm64 phones (Naqi), but only in offline two-pass mode; the absence of any published throughput numbers and the "about 2x free space" and "checkpointed processing for long videos" notes imply it is far slower than real-time on CPU. Real-time on-phone would need a lightweight causal model (speech-enhancement style, as NoNoiseYT uses) rather than a full stem separator.
- Because RootlessJamesDSP both requires DUMP/Shizuku and is incompatible with other DynamicsProcessing users, the most likely muting mechanism is attaching an effect to discovered session IDs to silence the original stream while the captured/processed copy is replayed; this is not confirmed by any source found (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No source documents exactly how RootlessJamesDSP silences the original app stream to avoid double audio (README, F-Droid, issues and third-party guides are silent; DeepWiki returned HTTP 429 twice). The inference above is circumstantial.
- "Flat Equalizer" could not be matched with certainty to a specific package; one review describes it as a free, open-source, system-wide 10-band EQ "not compatible with all apps or Android phones" without naming its method. Equalizer314 is documented instead; confirm the intended app before copying details.
- Halal | Music Remover (appriser): model, reviews and update date unverified (Play page truncated, no mirror found).
- NoMusic and NoNoiseYT do not disclose model names; no independent quality tests exist for any of the browser extensions (sources are vendor listings).
- No latency figures (ms) are published for any AudioPlaybackCapture-based app.

## Key Question 3: Does Samsung's Audio Eraser work in real time on arbitrary apps' audio, or only on files in Samsung apps? Is there any API for third-party apps? (Plus Pixel and Apple equivalents)

### Takeaway
Yes, since One UI 8.5 (Galaxy S26 launch, Feb 2026; backported to S25/S24/Z Fold 7-class devices from May 2026) Audio Eraser runs in real time on audio from third-party apps via a Quick-panel tile, but that mode exposes only a strength slider plus a "Voice Focus" toggle tuned for noise, not the per-stem Voices/Music/Wind/Nature/Crowd/Noise mixer available for files in Gallery; reviewers note it has no music- or vocal-specific mode. No third-party SDK/API for Audio Eraser was found. Pixel's Audio Magic Eraser and Apple's Audio Mix are file-edit features in Photos only (Audio Mix additionally requires Spatial Audio recorded on iPhone 16+), and Apple's Voice Isolation is a microphone mode for calls, not a playback filter.

### Inferences
- Samsung's real-time tile is the only shipping proof that on-device, system-wide, real-time speech-vs-background separation on arbitrary apps' audio (including DRM streams like Netflix) is feasible on a 2026 flagship NPU; but it is implemented inside One UI's privileged audio stack, so a third-party app cannot call it, and its model is a noise-suppression/voice-focus model that reviewers describe as leaving music in the "background sounds" bucket at reduced level rather than offering a "remove music" stem.
- Because the Gallery mode has an explicit "Music" stem but the live mode only has a strength slider, a Muslim user on a Galaxy S26 can mute music in saved files but can only *attenuate* mixed background (including music) during YouTube/Instagram playback, with artifacts.
- Pixel and Apple features are strictly file editors and (for Apple) depend on four-mic Spatial Audio capture metadata, so they are irrelevant for downloaded or streamed content; the Apple iOS 26 API cannot be used to build a system-wide playback filter.

- Samsung does not document whether live mode uses the same six-class model as Gallery, the model's latency, or battery impact; no reviewer quantified latency or battery.
- No source confirmed or denied an Audio Eraser SDK; absence of evidence only.
- The Apple iPhone User Guide page truncated before the Audio Mix section; the "iPhone 18 Pro isolates music" statement rests on a search-result summary of that page plus BGR.

## Key Question 4: What do users of these apps complain about (latency, echo/double audio, apps not captured, battery, quality artifacts)?

### Takeaway
Complaints cluster into five buckets: separation quality (instruments left in, speech words deleted when overlapping, "AI-processed"/sharpened consonants), sync/latency (HaramMute A/V drift, 5-10 s first-play waits, added latency on capture-based DSP), coverage holes (apps that opt out of capture such as Spotify/Chrome; YouTube hiding its session ID from EQ apps; Bluetooth A2DP offload bypassing effects), double/looped audio and dropouts (RootlessJamesDSP self-capture echo, session-0 contention), and UX intrusions (Samsung's un-hideable tile). Battery complaints were notably absent from the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- Quality, HaramMute: some instruments not detected on a recitation video; audio and picture misaligned (Play reviews); extension audio delay fixed by re-seeking (X reply). — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.harammute.mobile&hl=en); [HaramBlur on X](https://x.com/HaramBlur/status/2011856296056938760)

### Inferences
- For a system-wide instrument-muting app, the complaints predict the failure modes to design around: (1) opt-out apps and DRM video will silently bypass capture, so the UI must show "this app cannot be processed"; (2) any capture-and-replay design must guarantee the original stream is silenced and must exclude its own output from capture, or users will get echo; (3) users judge quality on consonant sharpness, sung lines in dialogue, and overlapping transients, which are exactly where current separators fail; (4) first-play buffering of several seconds is tolerated when cached (Music Remover) but A/V drift is not (HaramMute).
- The absence of battery complaints in sources is likely because none of the third-party apps run a neural model continuously on-device in real time; a continuous separator would be the first to surface that complaint.

### Gaps
- No user reports quantify battery drain or thermal throttling for any real-time audio AI on phones.
- No Reddit/XDA threads specifically about Islamic music-muting apps were found; the complaint evidence comes from store listings, GitHub issues, Samsung community posts and press hands-ons.
- Full review text for HaramMute (Play) and Halal | Music Remover could not be retrieved.

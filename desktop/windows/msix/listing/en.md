# Microsoft Store listing: English (en-us)

## Description

Waaiya removes musical instruments from everything your computer plays and keeps the voice: speech, lectures and
Quran recitation. It works with every app (browsers, video players, games), live, on your computer.

- One button: Waaiya filters the sound of all your other apps until you press it again.
- Links: paste a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link and watch it in your browser with the filter on. Waaiya starts the
  filter first and never opens the link without it.
- Mute women's singing (optional, for men): an early version that does not catch every song yet; speech is kept.
- Fails closed: if the filter stops by itself, other apps stay silent instead of playing music.
- Optional screen blur: covers women, or immodest images, over every window; clicks still reach the windows
  underneath.
- Start at login, if you want.
- Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere: no account, no analytics, no ads.
- Free and open source (AGPL-3.0)
- In 18 languages: Arabic, English, French, Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Bengali, Persian, Malay, Spanish, Dutch,
  German, Italian, Russian, Uzbek, Swahili, Hausa and Somali.

The name comes from the Quran: «وَتَعِيَهَا أُذُنٌ وَاعِيَةٌ», "and that a heedful ear would retain it" (al-Ḥāqqah 69:12).

It needs Windows 10 version 2004 or newer. A fast computer gives the best sound; on a slow one Waaiya switches to
mono by itself.

## What's new in this version

First version in the Microsoft Store.

# Waaiya: privacy policy

Waaiya does not collect, store or send any personal information. The sound filter and the screen blur run entirely
on your device; the sound and the screen are processed in memory and never recorded or sent anywhere. There is no
account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Network.** The Windows app from the Microsoft Store does not use the network. Its Links page opens a link you paste
in your own web browser; Waaiya itself does not contact the site. The Windows app from GitHub (the zip) and the
Android app can also save a link's video without music: then they contact the site of the link you opened to
download it, and nothing else. The Android app's download tool (yt-dlp) downloads its own updates from PyPI.

**What stays on your device.** Your settings (language, the options you turned on) and the sound filter's working
files (the apps' volume levels to give back, a short log of the filter's own messages, without any sound) are kept
on your device only: on Windows in `%APPDATA%\Waaiya` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Waaiya`, on Android in the app's own
storage. Windows does not delete these two folders when you uninstall the app; you can delete them yourself. Saved
videos go to the folder you see in the app, and are yours.

**Children.** Waaiya collects nothing from anyone, children included.

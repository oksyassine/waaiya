# Code of conduct

This project serves people who want to keep certain sounds out of their lives for religious reasons. Everyone
who takes part, whatever their background or beliefs, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, and assume good intent;
- discuss code, data and decisions, not people;
- not mock anyone's faith or religious practice, nor debate religious rulings in the issue tracker (the app
  gives settings, it does not issue rulings);
- not harass anyone, publicly or privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people, who do not follow this. Report problems privately to
the maintainers through GitHub (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability" also works for conduct reports).
